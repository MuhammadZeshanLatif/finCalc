# FinCalc - Financial & Multipurpose Calculators

This website offers high-performance financial, health, and date calculators in English and Russian.
Designed for personal finance planning, interest calculation, mortgage estimations, loan EMI calculation, and everyday tools like VAT and BMI.

## Core Calculators

1. Interest Calculator: Compound and simple interest calculators for long-term investments.
2. Deposit Calculator: High-yield savings accounts and fixed deposits yields.
3. Loan Calculator: Compare total interest payable on different terms.
4. Mortgage Calculator: Home mortgage estimator including downpayments and insurance.
5. Days Calculator: Date difference and work day calculations.
6. VAT Calculator: Inclusive and exclusive value added tax calculations.
7. EMI Calculator: Equated Monthly Installment for vehicle or home loans.
8. BMI Calculator: Metric and Imperial body mass index with recommendations.
9. Tax Calculator: Progressive income tax estimates.
10. Currency Converter: Live-simulated currency conversion rates.

Each calculator is bundled with educational financial content describing the formulas and applications.
Both English and Russian are fully supported. Use /ru for the Russian version.
