# FinanceCalculator keyword research and content plan (October 2026)

## How this list was built

- Seeds: the 14 calculators on the site (mortgage, loan, EMI, interest, deposit, tax, sales tax, VAT, currency, days, BMI, basic/scientific, TI-84, graphing).
- Expansion: question-style and "how much / how to calculate / vs" queries that a calculator answers, plus the yearly-limit queries (2026 brackets, contribution limits) that US searchers repeat every year.
- Filter: every topic needs a number the reader wants, so each article can include a worked example and a link to a calculator. Topics that need product reviews, live rate tables or legal opinions were dropped.
- Cannibalization check: the 17 built-in guides already own compound interest basics, mortgage mistakes, EMI prepayment, emergency fund + HYSA, progressive brackets/net income, travel currency, VAT inclusive/exclusive, TI-84 online, BMI and insurance, EMI formula, the Russia sales/NDFL guides, deposit, graphing calculator, loan monthly payment and mortgage payment guides. New drafts take different queries and link to those guides instead of repeating them.
- No keyword-volume tool was connected for this run (the OpenSEO connector needs authorization), so there are no search-volume or difficulty numbers here. Priority is a judgment from intent and how directly a calculator answers the query: **A** = high commercial/ad value and strong calculator fit, **B** = solid informational, **C** = supporting / long-tail.
- Audience: US first (Mediavine pays most for US, UK, CA, AU traffic). Figures come from `blog-drafts/_facts-2026.md`.

## Publishing advice

All 150 articles are saved as **drafts**. Publish slowly (for example 3–5 a week) after a human edit of each one: add a real example or opinion, check every number with the calculator, and confirm sources. Mass-publishing unedited AI-assisted articles is the fastest way to fail a Mediavine content review.
